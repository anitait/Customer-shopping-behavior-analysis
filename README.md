**Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis**

**Project Overview**

This project explores customer shopping behavior using transactional data from a retail company. The objective was to identify purchasing patterns, understand customer preferences, analyze customer segments and uncover insights that could help improve sales, customer engagement and customer loyalty.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, combining Python, PostgreSQL, SQL and Power BI to transform raw data into actionable business insights.

**Business Problem**

The company wanted to better understand its customers and identify the factors influencing their purchasing decisions.

The main business questions included:

- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- How do purchasing patterns differ across demographics?
- Which products and categories perform best?
- Do discounts influence purchasing behavior?
- How do subscribers compare to non-subscribers?
- What factors are associated with customer loyalty and repeat purchases?

**Dataset**

The dataset contains 3,900 records and 18 columns, covering different aspects of customer shopping behavior.

Key variables include:

- Customer demographics: Age, Gender, Location and Subscription Status.
- Purchase information: Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size and Color.
- Shopping behavior: Discount Applied, Previous Purchases and Frequency of Purchases.
- Customer experience: Review Rating and Shipping Type.

The dataset initially contained 37 missing values in the Review Rating column.

**Tools & Technologies**

- Python: Data cleaning, preprocessing and exploratory data analysis.
- Pandas: Data manipulation and transformation.
- PostgreSQL: Database management and storage of the cleaned dataset.
- SQL: Business analysis, aggregations, customer segmentation and querying.
- Power BI: Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.

**Project Workflow**

1. Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)

Started by importing the dataset into Python using Pandas and exploring its structure and characteristics.

Main steps:

- Loaded the dataset and examined its structure using df.info() and df.describe().
- Identified missing values and handled the 37 missing Review Rating values by imputing the median rating for each product category.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created an age_group variable to classify customers into different age segments.
- Created a purchase_frequency_days variable based on purchase frequency.
- Checked for redundancy between discount_applied and promo_code_used and removed the redundant column.
- Connected Python to PostgreSQL and transferred the cleaned dataset for further analysis.

2. Business Analysis (SQL / PostgreSQL)

Used SQL queries to investigate key business questions and identify patterns in customer purchasing behavior.

The analysis covered:

- Revenue by Gender: Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.
- High-Spending Discount Users: Identified customers who used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount.
- Top 5 Products by Rating: Identified products with the highest average customer review ratings.
- Shipping Type Comparison: Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.
- Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers: Compared customer numbers, average spending and total revenue according to subscription status.
- Discount-Dependent Products: Identified the five products with the highest proportion of discounted purchases.
- Customer Segmentation: Classified customers into New, Returning and Loyal segments based on their purchase history.
- Top 3 Products per Category: Identified the most frequently purchased products within each product category.
- Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions: Investigated the relationship between repeat purchasing (more than five purchases) and subscription status.
- Revenue by Age Group: Calculated the total revenue generated by each age group.

3. Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize the results and make the findings easier to interpret.

The dashboard includes:

- Key performance indicators (KPIs): Number of customers, average purchase amount and average review rating.
- Sales and revenue breakdown by product category.
- Customer distribution by subscription status.
- Revenue and sales by age group.
- Interactive filters for subscription status, gender, product category and shipping type.
- The dashboard allows users to explore customer behavior and compare different segments dynamically.

**Key Findings**

The analysis revealed several interesting patterns:

Customer Spending & Revenue

- The average purchase amount was $59.76.
- Male customers generated approximately $157,890 in revenue, compared with $75,191 generated by female customers.
- Clothing generated the highest sales and revenue among the four product categories, followed by Accessories, Footwear and Outerwear.

Subscription Behavior

- Approximately 27% of customers were subscribers, while 73% were non-subscribers.
- Subscribers generated $62,645 in total revenue, compared with $170,436 from non-subscribers.
- Average spending was relatively similar: $59.49 for subscribers and $59.87 for non-subscribers.

Product Performance

- Gloves, Sandals, Boots, Hats and Skirts were the five highest-rated products.
- Hats had the highest discount rate (50%), followed by Sneakers (49.66%) and Coats (49.07%).
- Jewelry was the most purchased product within Accessories, while Blouses led Clothing and Sandals led Footwear.

Customer Loyalty

- The customer segmentation identified 3,116 Loyal customers, 701 Returning customers and 83 New customers.
- Among customers with more than five purchases, 2,518 were non-subscribers and 958 were subscribers.

Shipping Preferences

- Customers using Express shipping had a slightly higher average purchase amount ($60.48) than those using Standard shipping ($58.46).

Age Groups

- Young Adults generated the highest revenue ($62,143), followed by Middle-aged customers ($59,197), Adults ($55,978) and Seniors ($55,763).

**Business Recommendations**

Based on the findings, several recommendations can be considered:

- Increase Subscription Adoption: Introduce exclusive benefits and incentives to encourage more customers to subscribe.
- Strengthen Customer Loyalty: Develop loyalty programs and targeted rewards to encourage repeat purchases.
- Review Discount Strategies: Evaluate discount effectiveness, particularly for products with high discount dependency, while considering profitability.
- Optimize Product Marketing: Highlight best-selling and highest-rated products in marketing campaigns.
- Improve Customer Targeting: Use demographic and purchasing insights to develop more targeted marketing strategies.
- Explore Shipping Preferences: Investigate the relationship between shipping options and purchasing behavior to improve the customer experience.
